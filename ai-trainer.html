A Complete AI-Powered TNPSC Prep Cycle

Ilachiyan AI Teacher combines previous year papers, AI-powered explanations, and exam-focused practice tools to make daily TNPSC preparation simpler, faster, and more effective. From practice to explanation to revision, it gives aspirants a complete cycle to improve speed, accuracy, and confidence for TNPSC Group 4 and other state competitive exams.

Every question comes with step-by-step AI reasoning in Tamil or English, audio playback, and progress tracking that resumes exactly where an aspirant stopped — built for real daily study habits, not one-off cramming.

AI Tutor Bilingual (Tamil/English) TNPSC Focused
What It Does

Aspirants practice year-wise previous papers with question-index tracking, so it's easy to see exactly how far they've progressed through each paper. Every question includes an AI explanation that walks through the reasoning and elimination strategy — not just the answer — available in both Tamil and English, with built-in audio playback for hands-free revision.

Beyond past papers, the app generates exam-style current affairs questions for ongoing revision, keeping preparation current between paper releases. The result is a single, structured loop — practice, explanation, audio revision, and current affairs — designed around how TNPSC and other state exam aspirants actually study day to day.

Key Deliverables

A mobile-first AI tutor built specifically for TNPSC and state competitive exam preparation, in Tamil and English.

  • Year-wise previous papers with answer keys
  • AI explanations with reasoning and elimination logic
  • Bilingual mode with question audio playback
  • Current affairs practice and progress tracking
