Most AI-driven products move from kickoff to a working v1 in 6–10 weeks. We run a short discovery sprint first to lock the roadmap, then build in reviewable cycles so you see progress every step of the way.
A clear problem statement, access to your existing systems (ecommerce store, ERP, CRM, or game backend), and a stakeholder who can make decisions. We kick off with a workshop to align on scope and success metrics.
We pick the right AI stack for your business — LLMs and automation for ecommerce and CRM workflows, or custom ML pipelines for ERP forecasting and game analytics — rather than forcing one-size-fits-all tech.
