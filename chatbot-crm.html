A Multi-Tenant AI Chatbot Platform, Under Your Brand

Letsdo Chatbot CRM is a multi-tenant SaaS product that helps businesses deploy an AI chatbot on their website, train it on their own content, capture qualified leads from live conversations, and manage follow-up from a central dashboard. It covers business onboarding, knowledge ingestion, embeddable widget delivery, real-time conversations, lead pipeline tracking, team access, workspace analytics, and billing — all under your own brand, ready to resell as your own chatbot SaaS.

Built on Next.js, PostgreSQL with pgvector, Prisma, and OpenAI, the platform is designed to be deployed and rebranded quickly, with Stripe or Razorpay billing built in from day one.

Multi-tenant SaaS AI chat widget Lead pipeline
What It Does

Businesses set up a workspace, define tone and escalation rules, and train the chatbot on website URLs, FAQs, notes, or uploaded documents — with crawl controls for which pages get included. The chat widget itself is fully brandable: custom name, logo, accent color, subtitle, and multilingual support, delivered as an embeddable widget on any site.

Every conversation feeds a lead pipeline — new, qualified, contacted, won, lost — with ownership and notes, so a sales or support team can pick up where the bot leaves off. Role-based access (owner, admin, staff) keeps teams organized, and a workspace dashboard surfaces usage: chats, leads, sources, domains, and plans.

Key Deliverables

A ready-to-deploy, white-label chatbot CRM your business or your clients can run under their own brand — from first website visit to a captured, qualified lead.

  • AI chatbot trained on your website, FAQs, and documents
  • Branded, embeddable chat widget with multilingual support
  • Lead pipeline with stages, ownership, and notes
  • Workspace analytics and role-based team access
